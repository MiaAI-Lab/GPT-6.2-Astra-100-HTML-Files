TITLE
Return to Olive Street

PROMPT
Lay a rose envelope on a pink #f4c9ba writing desk. Use plum #683937 engraved-looking rules and a large italic street address. Compose an intimate letter from a former neighbor about a fig tree, revealed when the envelope opens. Use an angular folded flap, postage-like perforations and a circular postmark. The envelope itself is the button. Lift the letter and fold the flap backward in sequence.

DESCRIPTION
A private piece of neighborhood history arrives as a tactile paper object.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Polygon paper folds, rotation about a horizontal hinge, layered postal typography.

INTERACTION MODEL
Open and close the envelope by activating it.
