TITLE
Penny Lock

PROMPT
Make a mint #c0dcce civil-engineering section of an invented canal lock in #3e6057 technical ink. Give the chamber hatched concrete abutments, a graduated depth gauge and a loaded red barge. A labelled valve switches between lower and upper pounds. Animate water and boat together and report the measured height on a giant digital-style readout. Lay the title on a narrow top rule like a waterworks drawing.

DESCRIPTION
A cutaway of Penny Lock elevates a loaded canal barge with one valve.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Section hatching, SVG translation, synchronized water level and floating hull, live gauge.

INTERACTION MODEL
Operate the sluice to fill or drain the chamber. Reduced motion changes levels immediately.
