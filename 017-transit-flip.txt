TITLE
Last Cars

PROMPT
Build the departures board of an invented tram depot. Amber #ffda70 mechanical flap letters sit in a wall of muted green-black #29342c modules, divided by horizontal hinge seams. Display one enormous destination, a smaller platform and time and a dispatch lever. Dispatching the car tumbles letters out of phase before the next destination settles. Make it feel like the last service of the night.

DESCRIPTION
Three late services rotate through a mechanical depot sign.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Split-flap shadows, staggered keyframes, monospaced display modules.

INTERACTION MODEL
Dispatch the waiting car to bring up the next service.
