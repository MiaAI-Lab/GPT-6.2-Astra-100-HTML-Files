TITLE
Under the Soot

PROMPT
Fill the viewport with a soft #272625 charcoal coating. Under it, engrave an intricate golden #efc97a sun as concentric spirographic rings on a dark field. Open with a torn diagonal streak already rubbed through. Put a restrained atelier mark and a rubbing instruction in the upper margin. Let fingers erase the surface freely and provide a keyboard-accessible broad polishing gesture. Static revelation replaces animated sparkle.

DESCRIPTION
A hidden metallic guilloché plate gradually comes to light under the visitor's hand.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Layered canvases, destination-out compositing, fine polar curves, stippled soot.

INTERACTION MODEL
Drag over the soot or press Polish to clear a stripe. Recoat restores the cover.
