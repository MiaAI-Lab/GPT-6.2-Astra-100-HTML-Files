TITLE
The Estuary at Low Water

PROMPT
Draw a full-width estuary chart on pale silt #ddd6aa with teal #376f75 water. Use a continuous mathematical elevation field to produce coherent contour lines and sandy ridges. Label an imaginary reach in compact survey lettering, with a wide tide gauge under the chart. Raising it should swallow sandbanks and split narrow spits into islands. State clearly that this is a model estuary. Keep the cartographic surface still until operated.

DESCRIPTION
A fictional shore changes geography as the model tide rises and falls.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Rasterized elevation field, contour bands, paper chart frame, gauge.

INTERACTION MODEL
Adjust the water height with the gauge to move the coastline.
