TITLE
The Hidden Colours of Ink

PROMPT
A pigment test bench on warm #e6dbc9 paper. Put a narrow column of typographic instructions beside a tall chromatography strip. Fill the strip with thousands of tiny translucent ink grains in separated teal, violet #4d4266 and ochre bands. A water-front slider lifts the bands by different distances, revealing several colours inside one dark starting blot. Use small calibrated numbers and an elegant serif title.

DESCRIPTION
A fictional ink called Widow Violet separates into three visible constituents as water rises.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Seeded pigment grain, absorbent paper shadow, graduated strip, variable capillary bands.

INTERACTION MODEL
Slide the water front to separate or recombine the ink.
