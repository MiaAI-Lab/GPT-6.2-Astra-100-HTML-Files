TITLE
Orchard Roll Call

PROMPT
Inventory a fictional orchard from above. On pear-skin #e0e5be arrange rows of clustered crowns in leaf green #4a6340, yellow and gray-green. Distinguish the ages through crown size and shape. Set a tall acreage number and a narrow ledger beside the aerial plot. Three age-band switches should light up the chosen trees, fade the rest, and count the standing trees in that band. Use an agronomist's matter-of-fact copy and plan lettering.

DESCRIPTION
An orchard census links a visual survey to the calculated count in each age band.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Canvas crown rosettes, staggered planting rows, plot boundaries, dynamic ledger.

INTERACTION MODEL
Select all trees or an age band to highlight its plantings and read the count.
