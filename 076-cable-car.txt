TITLE
The Far Station

PROMPT
Draw a Swiss-inspired excursion poster for the imaginary Veyre aerial line. Give most of the space to jagged lavender and plum ridges under a cool #bfdbed sky. Suspend a tiny red cabin from a diagonal wire; an ochre sun and bold tightly tracked black lettering anchor opposite corners. A boarding button should carry the car smoothly along the wire and switch destination at arrival. Keep the copy as terse as a printed excursion ticket.

DESCRIPTION
A return ticket between two otherwise inaccessible mountain stations doubles as a landscape poster.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Layered angular silhouettes, diagonal cable geometry, outlined cabin windows, restrained perspective.

INTERACTION MODEL
Board to travel to the opposite terminus. Reduced motion makes the transfer immediate.
