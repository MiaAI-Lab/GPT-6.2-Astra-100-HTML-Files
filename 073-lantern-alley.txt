TITLE
Lamplighter’s Round

PROMPT
Design a narrow paper street after dusk against #472b3b. Hang three large ribbed lanterns over layered crooked shopfronts. In warm #ffc483 light, give each house a distinct expression from its windows and roofline. Let a visitor tap a lantern to light it; a corresponding row of windows wakes below it. Use a compact handbill title and a quiet progress count. Avoid motion beyond a slow brightening.

DESCRIPTION
On Marrow Lane a little light passed from a lantern brings the houses to life.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Pleated lantern bodies, luminous windows, stepped paper facades, responsive light states.

INTERACTION MODEL
Toggle each lantern to wake or quiet its shopfront.
