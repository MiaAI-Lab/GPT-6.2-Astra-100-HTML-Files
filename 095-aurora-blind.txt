TITLE
Sleeper to North Haven

PROMPT
Build the window of a night train with a thick rounded metal frame, a layered green #a1e7cb aurora over indigo #1a2839 hills, and a partly shut Venetian blind. Use the quiet compact lettering of a railway berth. The tilt lever rotates the slats until the striped light becomes a whole horizon. Aurora curtains should move almost imperceptibly and remain still for reduced motion.

DESCRIPTION
A sleeper passenger gradually opens the blind onto a polar sky.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Perspective CSS slats, layered atmospheric gradients, clipped ridge silhouettes, inset frame.

INTERACTION MODEL
Move the tilt lever with touch or keyboard to reveal the full night landscape.
