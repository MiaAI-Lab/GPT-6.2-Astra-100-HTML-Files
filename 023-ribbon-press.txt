TITLE
Loose Register

PROMPT
Make an exuberant press proof on salmon #ffd8d0 with blue #255cad ink and a red-orange second plate. Fill a tall art sheet with intertwined broad loops, split by fine paper-colored racing stripes. Add crop marks and a rough edition imprint. Horizontally drag the red printing plate out of register, changing the topology of the overlapping colors. Set the name vertically by the art.

DESCRIPTION
A two-plate printer's proof makes the play between registration and abstraction tangible.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG ribbon paths, parallel keylines, multiply blending and registration crosses.

INTERACTION MODEL
Adjust horizontal register with the press slider.
