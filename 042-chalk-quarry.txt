TITLE
The Chalk Yard

PROMPT
Make a mint #bbdbd4 loading yard where thick chalk blocks travel on an overhead rail. Draw hard #43695c slab edges and dusty repeating scratches; let the stack balance on its overlap. A large drop key cuts away the unsupported end and sends the offcut tumbling out of sight. Show the height in courses and a compact quarry operator's instruction. Provide a still, manually adjustable carriage under reduced motion.

DESCRIPTION
A small masonry game asks the visitor to stack traveling chalk slabs with increasingly exact timing.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
DOM geometry, clipped overlaps, easing fall animation, chalk scratches.

INTERACTION MODEL
Drop the moving slab while it overlaps the stack. A manual position slider is also available. Restart after a miss or seven courses.
