TITLE
Wind Post

PROMPT
Design an aerogramme from a maple tree. Use #e9dfb6 stock, #785c39 botanical ink and narrow double borders. Scatter large translucent veined samaras across a stamp-like field, and let opening the packet release them into a slow tumbling descent. Give the packet a hand-set serif name, a dispatch address and concise instructions. The reduced-motion version should show the seeds at rest in their destination.

DESCRIPTION
Winged maple seeds become the letters of a wind-powered postal service.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Parametric canvas wings, vein strokes, staggered spinning descent, printed packet frame.

INTERACTION MODEL
Dispatch the seeds to watch them drift down. Press again to send another flight.
