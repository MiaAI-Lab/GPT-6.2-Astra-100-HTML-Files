TITLE
After the Overture

PROMPT
Stage a single sagging velvet rope between two brass stanchions in a theatrical #442035 lobby. Use #e9ab99 highlights in the pile and narrow italic title lettering near the floor. Plucking the rope sets off a damped wave that moves along its length; enable its low plucked note only through a visible sound toggle. Keep the foyer dark and the signage understated.

DESCRIPTION
A quiet instrument hides in the cordon outside an imaginary concert hall.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Layered canvas rope strokes, metallic post gradients, damped standing waves, synthesized pluck.

INTERACTION MODEL
Touch the rope or press the pluck button. Sound is independently opt-in; reduced motion settles quickly.
