TITLE
The Pocket Loom

PROMPT
Lay a half-woven striped band over a mass of upright warp strings on #ead1bb linen. Alternate berry #694643, brick, flax and gold in a fine twill. Show the wooden loom rails and a single pointed shuttle poised at the loose edge. Each throw inserts another patterned band and reverses shuttle direction. Use a woven-goods maker’s small serif signature and a practical pick count. Let visitors take the finished sample off and start over.

DESCRIPTION
A tiny handloom builds a patterned band one shuttle pass at a time.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Canvas warp and weft overlaps, procedural twill, wooden shuttle, printed selvedge.

INTERACTION MODEL
Throw the shuttle to advance the weave. Lift the completed swatch to reset the loom.
