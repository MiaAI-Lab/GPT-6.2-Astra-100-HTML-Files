TITLE
The Long Chord

PROMPT

A lever underneath opens the pleats and changes the chord’s register. Provide a separate sound toggle, silent at entry. Use compact capitals on the casing and a small sheet-music-like caption below. Let the instrument dominate the viewport.

A concertina study stretches a held chord across a room of red lacquer.
