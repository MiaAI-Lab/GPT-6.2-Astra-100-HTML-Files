TITLE
Rooms Behind Brick

PROMPT
Construct an entire symmetrical Georgian terrace elevation from brick red #d9a68a, mortar cream and soot #613c35. A chimney ridge and cornice cap rows of sash windows. Three bricked-up windows act as oral-history archives. Tapping one pulls away its masonry and reveals what an imaginary resident remembers. Use compact newspaper typography for the address and a broad cream transcript below the facade. Keep transitions short and architectural.

DESCRIPTION
A fictional house at Bracken Quay keeps testimony in windows that have been filled with brick.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Repeating masonry, inset sash shadows, architectural cornice, removable brick infill.

INTERACTION MODEL
Choose a sealed window to open it and read the resident’s recollection.
