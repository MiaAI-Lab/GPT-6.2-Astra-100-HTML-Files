TITLE
The Patient Egg

PROMPT
Make a small balancing game with a speckled sage egg on a long thin beam. Use a generous field of #c7dbbb and dark #496451 marks. Put the beam’s triangular support at center and show the egg rocking on the slope. A tilt control lets the reader brake a gently rolling egg and settle it within a painted nest at midpoint. Show a held-balance timer and provide reset. Write instructions like a patient kitchen experiment. For reduced motion allow direct placement.

DESCRIPTION
Counter-tilt a beam until the egg has nearly stopped in the painted nest.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Speckled CSS egg, tilting beam, damped rolling motion, timed balance detection.

INTERACTION MODEL
Use the slider to control the slope. Hold the egg in the center for two seconds. Reduced motion provides a position control.
