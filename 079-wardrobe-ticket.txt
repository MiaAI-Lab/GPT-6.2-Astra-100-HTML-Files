TITLE
Unclaimed at the Lyric

PROMPT
Give an abandoned theatre cloakroom the intimacy of a short story. Center an oversized perforated ticket with olive #49503d ink on faded #c3c9ae card. Set LYRIC in stately serif caps and an enormous claim number beside a vertically torn stub. Redeeming a ticket should tear away the stub and reveal a specific keepsake discovered in its coat. Allow three tickets to be examined in sequence.

DESCRIPTION
Three coats from the Lyric's final performance tell three miniature stories through the things left in their pockets.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Punched paper holes, perforation, letterpress borders, sliding claim stub.

INTERACTION MODEL
Redeem the ticket and read the pocket inventory. Draw another claim to advance the narrative.
