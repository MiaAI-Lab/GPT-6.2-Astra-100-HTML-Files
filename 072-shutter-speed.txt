TITLE
One Turn of the Dancer

PROMPT
Design a movement laboratory plate in dusty rose #e5cbd4 and mulberry #593850. Place a dancer’s strong gesture along a horizontal path; layer translucent previous poses behind the leading figure. Use articulated limbs, a circular head and flared skirt instead of a stick figure. The exposure slider should move from a single frozen figure to a readable fan of motion. Frame the plate with tiny typographic exposure marks and an asymmetric heading.

DESCRIPTION
A fictional studio test records a dancer’s traveling turn as a variable multiple exposure.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Articulated vector silhouettes, opacity blending, successive poses, photographic tick marks.

INTERACTION MODEL
Lengthen the exposure to see earlier poses accumulate.
