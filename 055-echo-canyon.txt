TITLE
Four Returns

PROMPT
Make a panoramic woodcut canyon in rust #a64737, amber #ffdb9a, black plum and clay. Crowd the horizon with broken mesas and hatch their walls. Place four echo stations at progressively higher ledges. A single explicit sound button sends a short sung note across the ravine. Stage diminishing rings at successively distant stations and schedule quieter audio returns. Use the spare lettering of a Western record sleeve.

DESCRIPTION
A canyon answers a single call four times, each return smaller than the last.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Layered mesa profiles, hatched rock, staggered expanding line rings, synthesized tones.

INTERACTION MODEL
Press the sound button to call into the canyon. Four echoes travel across the illustration.
