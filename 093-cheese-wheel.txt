TITLE
Wheel No. 12

PROMPT

A tasting iron extracts one of three samples, each tagged with its location and a particular flavor note. Pair dark #75452c serif numerals with small dairy ledger labels. The wheel is fictional and should feel like a remembered family farm.

DESCRIPTION
Three core samples trace the imagined flavor of an alpine wheel from rind to center.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Isometric wheel silhouette, stippled rind hatching, cheese eyes, sliding tasting iron.

INTERACTION MODEL
