TITLE
Mail Along the Sound

PROMPT
Place an oversized landscape postcard on a seafoam #c4e0dd post-office counter. The reverse has a divided address panel, a perforated lighthouse stamp, written message and round #365960 cancellation. Three coastal office buttons change the handwritten message, destination cancellation and serial. Animate each cancellation falling onto the stamp like a heavy date punch. Use stamp engraving, wide serif address lines and very little interface copy.

DESCRIPTION
Three imaginary coastal offices stamp and dispatch a single postcard with local harbor news.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
CSS postage perforations, engraved lighthouse SVG, circular cancellation, paper rotation.

INTERACTION MODEL
Choose a harbor to change its message and apply a fresh cancellation.
