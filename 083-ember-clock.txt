TITLE
A Minute of Ash

PROMPT
Make a live clock from sixty irregular pieces of charcoal, arranged as six low rows on a #312421 hearth. Pieces already reached by the local minute glow #ffc087 at their fractured centers; those ahead remain cool grey. Display the actual local time in slender, very large serif numerals above the coals, and a legend below. At the turn of each minute let the fire bed empty and begin again. No controls or ornamental motion.

DESCRIPTION
Sixty coals make the passing minute tangible, one new ember at a time.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Deterministically faceted charcoal polygons, inner glow gradients, clock-synchronized rendering.

INTERACTION MODEL
Watch the local time. Each second ignites a coal, with a new bed at the minute boundary.
