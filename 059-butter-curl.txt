TITLE
Beurre en Copeaux

PROMPT
Create an illustrated dairy wrapper in butter yellow #ffe898 and toasted ink #82521d. Give it scalloped brown borders, curved script-like Georgia italics and a central isometric block of cultured butter on wax paper. A grooved shaving appears beside the block every time the reader draws the butter curler. Reduce the block and stack the curls neatly. Use the language of a small fictional creamery.

DESCRIPTION
A paper wrapper doubles as the serving counter at Bell Meadow Dairy.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Three-face vector block, ridged curl symbols, scalloped wrapper edges.

INTERACTION MODEL
Shave up to eight curls and unwrap another block.
