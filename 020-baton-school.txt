TITLE
The Silent Downbeat

PROMPT
Place an ivory #f4dbab conducting diagram on a wine #46212e rehearsal-room wall. A single baton follows the four beats of common time, down, in, out, up, across a gracefully curved path. Mark the endpoints with delicate numbered rings. A viewer advances one beat per tap, visibly moving the baton and illuminating the current count. Use italic musical Italian and engraved rehearsal typography.

DESCRIPTION
A silent studio teaches a four-beat gesture one movement at a time.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Curved SVG diagram, animated coordinates, pivoting tapered baton.

INTERACTION MODEL
Press the beat key repeatedly to conduct a bar.
