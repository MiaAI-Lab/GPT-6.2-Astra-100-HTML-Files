TITLE
The Vanishing Disc

PROMPT
Set a hot peach #ffb875 sun in a nearly black plum #261c27 sky. Use tangles of radial rays and several soft rings to build the corona, then lay an opaque moon in front. A continuous alignment control moves the silhouette into totality. At center let fine long streamers emerge and change the observing log to totality. Keep the heading small and observatory-like so the luminous disc dominates. Do not flash the screen.

DESCRIPTION
A handheld occultation model reveals a solar corona at exact alignment.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Radial CSS corona, conic rays, moving occluder, live separation readout.

INTERACTION MODEL
Use the slider to align the two bodies and reveal the outer corona.
