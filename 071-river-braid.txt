TITLE
Channels of the Vey

PROMPT
Treat the braided fictional Vey River as an unbound topographic map. On tan silt #d8c9ac draw dozens of crossing slate-water #52757b channels and parallel dry banks. Place map lettering directly on the landscape and a compact diversion lever in its southwest corner. As the reader diverts flow, some channels spread while others narrow. Let the raised bars and islands read as an organic woven pattern.

DESCRIPTION
A river survey follows the paths that water could take through a wide gravel plain.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Parametric channel centerlines, silt texture, multilayer bank strokes, map annotations.

INTERACTION MODEL
Move the diversion lever to redistribute the river threads.
