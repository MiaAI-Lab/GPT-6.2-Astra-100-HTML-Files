TITLE
Amber Fold

PROMPT
Create an abstract optical object with a hand-written fragment shader: an eccentric disc of smoked #302935 glass with soft folds of liquid #dfa872 caustic light. Cover the viewport, placing quiet engraved serif lettering near one corner. A control changes the angle of the folds like rotating a lens in the hand. Provide a static CSS optical disc where WebGL is unavailable, and keep the field still until adjusted.

DESCRIPTION
A dark glass lens catches a narrow amber beam and splits it into nested arcs.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Signed radial coordinates, layered trigonometric caustics, shader vignette, procedural fallback.

INTERACTION MODEL
Turn the range control to revolve the refracted bands inside the glass.
