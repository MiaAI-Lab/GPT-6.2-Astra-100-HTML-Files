TITLE
The Shore Reads Both Ways

PROMPT
Lay out an original reversible shore poem as a long vertical tide gauge. Alternate left- and right-bank lines on mist #d5dee4 with blue ink #344e67. Make a fixed waterline track the reader’s depth and quietly tint the submerged paper. Reading down gives an arrival at the sea; reading upward leads back to the house. Use refined serif fragments, large empty intervals and a sticky gauge for the tide. Finish with a return-to-top control.

DESCRIPTION
A walk between kitchen and sea can be read in either direction.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Vertical reading sequence, mirrored margins, fixed translucent wash, live tide gauge.

INTERACTION MODEL
Scroll down the poem, then read upward with the changing tide. The final link returns to the beginning.
