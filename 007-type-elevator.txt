TITLE
Point House

PROMPT
Design an acid-paper #e7edcf typographer's lift with #222e2b ink. Place a tall open shaft of enormous lowercase g specimens alongside four square floor buttons. Move the entire car through the shaft with overshooting elevator easing. Each floor demonstrates a different type voice and announces its name. Use a monumental numeric floor indicator and precise machine labels.

DESCRIPTION
Four storeys of a fictional type foundry house four different lowercase residents.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Masked vertical specimen strip, display-size glyphs, inset control panel.

INTERACTION MODEL
Choose a floor to ride to its specimen.
