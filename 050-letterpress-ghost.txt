TITLE
The Ghost Impression

PROMPT
Design a letterpress proof sheet on fibrous ivory #eeebd9. Print the word ECHO on four lines, growing in weight and tracking, with a visible coral ghost displaced over a blue #1d4967 impression. Include corner registration targets and a job line from a fictional press. A horizontal registration screw should converge both plates exactly, darkening the overlap and reporting the offset on a live galley readout. Use the word itself as the image.

DESCRIPTION
A print proof makes the thin boundary between a ghost and a clean impression legible.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG overprinted type, multiply blending, crosshair registration marks, varying letter spacing.

INTERACTION MODEL
Turn the registration control toward zero to bring the two impressions together.
