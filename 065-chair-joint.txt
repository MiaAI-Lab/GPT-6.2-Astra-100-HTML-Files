TITLE
Shoulder & Cheek

PROMPT
Illustrate a mortise and tenon as an exploded furniture-maker’s plate on #d6bd98 stock. Make a thick upright walnut post with a dark rectangular mortise and a horizontal rail with a narrow tongue. Draw grain lines along each plane with #493b2e ink. A fitted/exploded control retracts the rail along a dashed alignment guide. Use a sober serif heading and workshop annotations naming the shoulder and cheek.

DESCRIPTION
A chair rail parts from its post to expose the joint normally hidden inside.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Axonometric solids, incised grain, alignment guide, sliding joinery.

INTERACTION MODEL
Fit the rail into the post and withdraw it again.
