TITLE
Shelf of Small Silences

PROMPT
Design the shelf of a fictional lending library devoted to silence, with five differently proportioned cloth spines in dusty rose #d3bdbe, plum #534755 and faded sage. Let each title read vertically in engraved serif type; include double gold rules and individual shelf marks. Select a book to lift it above the shelf and display its first sentence on the catalogue slip below. Use a literary rather than technical voice.

DESCRIPTION
A pocket library describes kinds of silence by way of the opening lines of imagined books.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Vertical lettering, cloth-like microstripes, foil rules, individually proportioned spines.

INTERACTION MODEL
Press a spine to pull it forward and read the selected opening on the slip.
