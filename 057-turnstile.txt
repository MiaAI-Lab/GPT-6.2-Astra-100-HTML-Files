TITLE
One Body Through

PROMPT
Build a severe municipal admission counter in mint #d6e4ca and dark green #374d46. Give the top third to an enormous machine number with leading zeroes. Below it diagram a top-down three-armed turnstile between two striped barriers. Each admission rotates the gate through precisely one third of a turn and increments the mechanical total. Name each fictional passenger in a little station log. Use heavy grotesque numerals and compact monospace directions.

DESCRIPTION
An imaginary station records its morning arrivals one passenger at a time.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Large tabular counter, striped barriers, threefold rotor, mechanical easing.

INTERACTION MODEL
Admit a passenger with the button and read the latest entry.
