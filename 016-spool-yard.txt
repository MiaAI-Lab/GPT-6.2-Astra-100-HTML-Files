TITLE
The Winding Room

PROMPT
Draw a huge wooden bobbin in profile on apricot #ffd1a4. Thousands of winding diagonal filaments should build a dense skein of mulberry #83354a. Two wooden end flanges contain the thread. Let a skein-length slider wind and unwind the spool, exposing bare wood. Compose it like a haberdashery advertisement, with a bold tiny maker's mark and fine italic display lettering.

DESCRIPTION
A thread merchant's sample book lets the viewer wind a richly textured bobbin.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Canvas woodgrain, elliptical end caps, seeded filament drawing and highlights.

INTERACTION MODEL
Change the wound length with the yardage slider.
