TITLE
Fair Portions

PROMPT
Make a bakery table for sharing one round cherry pie. Arrange the pie as a large overhead engraving with a scalloped crust, golden diagonal lattice and red filling; leave a flour-colored #eed4aa margin around the copper #924b30 ink. Use a broad Georgia masthead and small recipe-book numerals. Pluses and minuses choose two to eight guests. The pastry should fan into mathematically equal sections with the lattice continuous across the cut and a changing fraction in the bill of fare.

DESCRIPTION
A round cherry pie becomes an edible fraction diagram as diners join the table.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG wedge clipping, continuous diagonal pastry pattern, scalloped perimeter, exploded geometry.

INTERACTION MODEL
Use the guest controls to divide the pie and read one diner's exact share.
