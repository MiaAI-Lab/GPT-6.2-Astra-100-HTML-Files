TIDE GAUGE / 00
POEM FOR AN INCOMING TIDE

The shore reads
both ways.

Walk down to the water. Read home against the tide.

The kettle cooling.

A latch still warm
from somebody’s hand.

Five steps of slate.

The lane narrows
to a thread of salt.

A rope darkens.

Between the posts,
another kind of sky.

The gull gives back
its borrowed shadow.

Water, everywhere.
