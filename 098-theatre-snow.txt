TITLE
Cue Thirty-Two

PROMPT
Seat the audience in a miniature theater with gold #efddc3 proscenium moldings and a dark #39405a cyclorama. Layer spare winter birches and painted hill flats behind the apron. A stage-manager's cue button releases square paper snow from the flies. Flakes should wobble as they fall, filling the depth of the scene without covering the control. Use precise prompt-book copy and small-cap signage.

DESCRIPTION
A winter set waits in silence until the stage manager drops paper snow across the painted landscape.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Nested proscenium borders, canvas cutout scenery and birch marks, tumbling finite snow particles.

INTERACTION MODEL
Call the snow cue to start a short fall. Calling again restarts it. Reduced motion gives a settled snowy tableau.
