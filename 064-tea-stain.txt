TITLE
Aunt Nella’s Saucer

PROMPT
Lay a porcelain saucer on #e9d4b1 tea paper with a circular tide mark in #7c492c. Design the ring as many translucent irregular strokes, leaf fragments settled along it. Set the fortune inside the cup margin in small italic serif type. Turn the cup a third at a time and let the leaves form a new reading named for a mundane household object. A warm, gentle parlor game, with a restrained invitation under the saucer.

DESCRIPTION
Aunt Nella reads small domestic futures in the leaves of a half-empty cup.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Uneven stain contours, leaf fragments, concentric china rim, rotating vector paths.

INTERACTION MODEL
Turn the saucer to reveal the next reading.
