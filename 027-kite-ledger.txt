TITLE
East of the Dunes

PROMPT
Design a kite maker's sky-wide test record with a red #9b382d swallowtail high over a pale turquoise #a8e3e2 beach. Draw ruled wind traces and a looping tail made of many tiny bows. Print a small monospace field entry low on the horizon, with a large sans number identifying the flight. Adjust the bridle on a ruled slider. As its angle changes, the paper kite should bank and the bows should follow its streaming tail. Keep the reduced-motion sky still between adjustments.

DESCRIPTION
A printed coastal field record lets visitors tune the pitch of a kite under steady wind.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
HiDPI canvas, articulated ribbon marks, ruled field typography.

INTERACTION MODEL
Move the bridle slider with touch, pointer or keys to alter the kite pitch and line.
