TITLE
The Keyless Works

PROMPT
Lay out a dismantled clock movement on aged brass #cec7a6. Two overlapping engraved wheels with cutaway spokes and radial teeth carry red witness marks. The player advances the right wheel one tooth at a time until its mark points directly at the other. Include small pivot screws, a numerical alignment reading and the clockmaker's one-line instruction in brown #554a34. Correct alignment should visibly seat the locking pin.

DESCRIPTION
A clockmaker's alignment exercise turns a pair of brass gears into a compact puzzle.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Parametric gear outlines, circular spoke apertures, animated angular stepping and locking pin.

INTERACTION MODEL
Advance the right wheel until the witness lines meet. Reset the alignment to practice again.
