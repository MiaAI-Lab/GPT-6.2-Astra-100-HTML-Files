TITLE
The North Pitch

PROMPT
Present a field report on a miniature ecosystem inhabiting an invented cottage roof. Place a steep slope of overlapping slate scales across the lower half of a moss-yellow #d0d3a7 field. Illustrate lichen coins, moss cushions and stonecrop in deep #485d40 ink. Above the slope run an editorial serif headline and a short field observation. Three habitat tabs relocate a circular specimen lens and replace the note with a specific observation.

DESCRIPTION
A botanical field note uses a single roof as a map of three small worlds.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Overlapping slate pattern, stippled specimen clusters, circular enlarged botanical detail.

INTERACTION MODEL
Select a habitat name to examine its specimen and read the corresponding field note.
