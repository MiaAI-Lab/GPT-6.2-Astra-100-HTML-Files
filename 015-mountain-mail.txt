TITLE
Pass Post

PROMPT
Draw a folded alpine postal route map with nested brown #5d483b contour loops on sage #d0debc. Mark three high passes with tiny station circles and route names. A route selector inks the line from the depot to the selected pass. Use the lean lettering of a topographic sheet, a compact scale and an oversized issue number.

DESCRIPTION
A mountain post office maintains three precarious routes across a landscape of contoured peaks.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Parametric contour lines, animated SVG dash offsets, station cartography.

INTERACTION MODEL
Choose a pass from the route list to trace its delivery path.
