TITLE
Supper Under Seal

PROMPT
Set a confidential supper menu as a large cream #e3d3ba restaurant broadside with severe #322f2d censor bars across the dish names. Use blackletter-like Georgia italics for the masthead and a red diagonal release stamp. Keep course headings tiny and aligned on a narrow left rail. Activating each bar rolls it off its dish with a sharp peeling motion. Write four courses with precise and slightly clandestine flavors.

DESCRIPTION
A sealed banquet record gives up its menu one course at a time.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Oversized serif menu, irregular ink bars, paper grain, stamped authorization.

INTERACTION MODEL
Activate a black bar to reveal its dish or reseal it.
