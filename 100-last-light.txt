TITLE
The Last Light

PROMPT
Make the closing exhibit a folding panorama of dusk. Spread angular lilac and oxblood headlands below a pale apricot #f0bb92 sky, with an enormous low sun behind the far ridge. Divide the landscape into vertical accordion folds whose shading alternates. Pull a final control to contract the entire panorama into a narrow spine, letting the simple parting line appear around it. Use very little ink, in muted #70495b, and almost no furniture.

DESCRIPTION
The final view of the exhibition folds up like a travel keepsake and opens again at a touch.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Continuous layered SVG panorama, repeated fold shading, eased horizontal contraction, disappearing sun.

INTERACTION MODEL
Close the panorama to reveal its farewell. Unfold it to restore the full landscape. Reduced motion jumps between states.
