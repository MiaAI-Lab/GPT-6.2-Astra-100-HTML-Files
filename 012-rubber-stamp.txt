TITLE
Office of Unusual Parcels

PROMPT
Build a cargo inspection docket on kraft #f0dcc2 paper with crimson #a53741 ink. Present three absurd imports in large slabby type, including a jar of distant thunder. The user admits a parcel by stamping anywhere on the docket; a ragged double-ring impression lands askew at their chosen position. Another consignment can be drawn up from the manifest. Use stacked bureaucratic headings and a ruled freight form.

DESCRIPTION
A fictional border office entrusts the viewer with its official seal.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Layered stamp impressions, paper rules, oversized docket typography.

INTERACTION MODEL
Tap the docket to stamp it or activate it with the keyboard. Advance through the manifest with the next button.
