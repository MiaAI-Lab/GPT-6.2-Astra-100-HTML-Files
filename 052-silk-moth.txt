TITLE
Into the Wick

PROMPT
Stage a solitary silk moth in olive midnight #242a20 with parchment wings #dcdaab and a single amber lamp. Use fine italic Georgia for a naturalist’s title tucked in the corner. Build a symmetrical scalloped insect with eyespots, veins, hairy antennae and abdomen. The light should move to a touched position and the moth should glide after it as if drawn by thread. Leave most of the field empty. Provide a button to move the lamp between perches.

DESCRIPTION
An imaginary specimen drifts through a pool of lamplight in an otherwise silent room.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Mirrored vector wings, engraved veins, radial illumination, eased translation.

INTERACTION MODEL
Tap the room or move the lamp with the perch button. The moth follows.
