TITLE
The Breakwater Test

PROMPT
Draw a coastal engineer’s wave tank from above in #b5cbd0, #384451 and white foam. Put an oversized narrow sans title over a ruled survey margin. Stack fine oscillating wave crests across the water. Let visitors tap the seabed to sink granite blocks that cast widening calm shadows downstream. Provide a keyboard placement control and a way to clear the wall. Use terse field instructions and coordinates.

DESCRIPTION
A working coastal sketch shows how a line of granite interrupts an incoming swell.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Procedural wave crests, angular granite silhouettes, downstream attenuation.

INTERACTION MODEL
Tap the tank to place granite or extend the wall with the keyboard-accessible button. Clear the tank to begin again.
