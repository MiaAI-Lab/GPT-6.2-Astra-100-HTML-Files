TITLE
Academy of Waiting

PROMPT
Build a leader for an imaginary film called The Long Interval. Place the oversized countdown number behind two fine circles and a crosshair inside a gray-green #e2dfca film frame. The film strip has dark #343330 perforated rails and a clipped lab inscription. Cranking through frames should sweep a translucent sector around the dial, advance the numeral and expose the hidden film title at the last frame. Use spare block type and an operator key, stopping between advances.

DESCRIPTION
A hand-operated countdown turns a few seconds of waiting into a private film premiere.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Perforated CSS rails, SVG target frame, radial shutter rotation, display numerals.

INTERACTION MODEL
Advance the reel with each key press. Rewind after the final title frame.
