TITLE
Cinder, Passing

PROMPT
Make a dark full-bleed comet engraving in #19232e with chalk white, ice #c8ede2 and copper trails. Write the title like a quiet astronomical marginal note at the bottom. Sweep hundreds of fine filaments across the sky into a tiny brilliant nucleus. Moving or tapping the nucleus’s destination steers the filaments in curved paths. A keyboard command should choose a new course. Keep the heavens spare and silent.

DESCRIPTION
An icy visitor turns its plumed tail across a private patch of sky.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Layered Bézier streamers, sparse seeded stars, high-DPI canvas, luminous nucleus.

INTERACTION MODEL
Point at the sky or press the course button to redirect the comet.
