TITLE
Combing the Fen

PROMPT
Create an edge-to-edge print of a marsh in late afternoon. Golden straw #e4d996 and olive #596938 dominate. Sweep hundreds of ink-thin drooping reed stalks across the lower field, with heavy cattail heads and tiny leaves. Touch or brush through the plants to push a bending wave through them. Put the title vertically down one edge like a printer's signature.

DESCRIPTION
An animated landscape responds to the visitor like a field brushed by a passing hand.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Depth-ranked canvas curves, tapered leaf strokes, local displacement field.

INTERACTION MODEL
Move or touch the field to bend reeds. Arrow keys move the breeze.
