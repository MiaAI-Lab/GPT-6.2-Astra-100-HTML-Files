TITLE
The Errata Room

PROMPT
Set a lexicographer's correction sheet almost entirely in type on pale #e9e1cc. A giant serif headword, numbered folio, phonetic aside and literary definition should resemble an old dictionary leaf. A red #99453e handwritten proof mark corrects a single letter and changes the whole sense. Offer three distinct misprints, each with an unexpected but plausible definition before and after correction. Keep the correction action at the word itself.

DESCRIPTION
Three entries in an imaginary dictionary turn on the smallest possible editorial intervention.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Typographic hierarchy, contrasted italic gloss, inline proof mark, ruled folio.

INTERACTION MODEL
Activate the marked character to correct or restore the word. Turn the leaf for another entry.
