TITLE
A City in the Margins

PROMPT
Make an annotated literary fragment set in enormous Georgia type on #ebe5cd paper. Three superscript buttons in #573b54 reveal streets in a city that exists only in footnotes. Set a narrow margin column alongside the fragment on wide screens. Treat the annotation like an atlas inset, with a changing orthogonal street plan, a numbered title and one precise memory. Changes should feel like turning a leaf, with a short ink reveal. Keep all three notes accessible on touch.

DESCRIPTION
A fictional walking passage opens into three small places as its footnotes are explored.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Oversized book typography, SVG street plan, inset marginalia, ink reveal.

INTERACTION MODEL
Select any superscript to replace the marginal map and text with its corresponding location.
