TITLE
The Matchbox Hotel

PROMPT
Stage a single enormous diagonal matchbox on a salmon #cc6550 counter. Print a scalloped black #251f22 label with a monumental H, an old hotel name and three tiny stars. Render a stippled striker on the long edge. Slide the ivory drawer sideways with a room key; inside each drawer is a short eccentric guest record. Use sturdy display serif lettering and typewritten guest cards. The drawer should settle like cardboard rubbing cardboard.

DESCRIPTION
A pocket-sized hotel hides an eccentric register under its commercial label.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Layered cardboard shadows, printed medallion, radial striker stipple, sliding drawer.

INTERACTION MODEL
The desk key alternates opening and closing the drawer, rotating among three guest records.
