TITLE
An Hour Under Linen

PROMPT
Make a side-by-side proofing journal with a handwritten-style time notation and a large scored loaf under raking light. Use honey #e9cf9d and toasted brown #6d3926. The bread should have a blistered crust, flour dust and four slashes; moving a proofing time control swells its silhouette and opens the slashes. Write the notes in a baker's first-person voice.

DESCRIPTION
A bakery workbench records a loaf's changes over a fictional hour of proofing.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
CSS radial crust, repeated surface flecks, stretched elliptical scoring, scale interpolation.

INTERACTION MODEL
Slide through the hour to see the loaf swell and read the baker's note.
